Unit 4: Financial Mathematics

12.1 — Perpetuity: key formulas, worked examples and exam-practice solutions.

Back to Applied Maths

Quick revision

Formula recap

Ordinary perpetuity: \(PV=\frac{R}{i}\)

Perpetuity due: \(PV=R+\frac{R}{i}=R\left(1+\frac{1}{i}\right)\)

Periodic rate: \(i=\frac{r}{m}\), so nominal annual rate \(r=mi\).

Here, \(R\) is the payment per period, \(i\) is the interest rate per payment period, and \(PV\) is present value.

Practice with solutions

Perpetuity worked examples

Use the formula and match the interest period to the payment period.

1. Annual perpetuity

A perpetuity pays ₹900 annually at 5% per annum. Find its present value.

\(PV=\frac{900}{0.05}=18,000\)

Answer: ₹18,000

2. Quarterly payments

A perpetuity pays ₹600 at the end of every quarter. The nominal annual rate is 6%, convertible quarterly.

Quarterly rate: \(i=0.06/4=0.015\).

\(PV=\frac{600}{0.015}=40,000\)

Answer: ₹40,000

3. Perpetuity due

A payment of ₹300 is made at the beginning of every six-month period. The annual nominal rate is 6%, convertible half-yearly.

Half-yearly rate: \(i=0.06/2=0.03\).

\(PV=300+\frac{300}{0.03}=300+10,000\)

Answer: ₹10,300

4. Semi-annual perpetuity

A perpetuity pays ₹18,000 every six months at 8% per annum, compounded semi-annually.

Half-yearly rate: \(i=0.08/2=0.04\).

\(PV=\frac{18,000}{0.04}\)

Answer: ₹4,50,000

5. Payment from an investment

₹72,000 is invested at 8% per annum, compounded half-yearly. Find the half-yearly interest payment.

Half-yearly rate: \(i=0.08/2=0.04\).

Payment: \(72,000\times0.04\)

Answer: ₹2,880 every six months

6. Find the nominal annual rate

A perpetuity pays ₹300 quarterly and has a present value of ₹24,000. Find the nominal annual rate convertible quarterly.

Quarterly rate: \(i=\frac{300}{24,000}=0.0125=1.25\%\).

Nominal annual rate: \(r=4(1.25\%)\).

Answer: 5% per annum, convertible quarterly

7. Annual scholarship

A scholarship pays ₹3,000 annually forever. At 5% per annum, find the fund required.

\(PV=\frac{3,000}{0.05}\)

Answer: ₹60,000

8. Scholarship at 8%

A scholarship pays ₹5,000 annually forever. Find its present value at 8% per annum.

\(PV=\frac{5,000}{0.08}=62,500\)

Answer: ₹62,500

9. Quarterly perpetuity rate

A perpetuity pays ₹7,500 quarterly and has a present value of ₹2,50,000. Find the nominal annual rate convertible quarterly.

Quarterly rate: \(i=\frac{7,500}{2,50,000}=0.03=3\%\).

Nominal annual rate: \(r=4(3\%)\).

Answer: 12% per annum, convertible quarterly

10. Semi-annual perpetuity rate

A perpetuity pays ₹1,500 every six months and has a present value of ₹20,000. Find the nominal annual rate compounded semi-annually.

Half-yearly rate: \(i=\frac{1,500}{20,000}=0.075=7.5\%\).

Nominal annual rate: \(r=2(7.5\%)\).

Answer: 15% per annum, compounded semi-annually

Exam check: Always convert the annual rate into the rate per payment period before applying the perpetuity formula. A payment at the beginning of each period is a perpetuity due; a payment at the end is an ordinary perpetuity.
